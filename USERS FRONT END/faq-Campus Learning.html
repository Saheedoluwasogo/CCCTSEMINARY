    Asked Questions

    Below are answers to the questions we are asked most often about admission, study and student life at the Celestial Church of Christ Theological Seminary. If your question is not covered here, please contact the seminary office and we will be glad to help.

    Who can apply to study at the seminary?
    Admission is open to ministers, church workers and committed members of the Celestial Church of Christ, as well as others called into ministry. Applicants should have a sincere desire to grow in the knowledge of God's word and service.
    How do I register for courses?
    Once admitted, sign in to the Students Portal with your matriculation number, choose the courses for the semester and confirm your registration. Course fees can be paid directly from your dashboard.
    How are lectures delivered?
    Lectures are delivered on campus and supported online. Lecture notes, slides and recordings published by your lecturers appear under "My Lectures & Materials" in your student dashboard for every course you are registered for.
    How do I pay my fees?
    Your outstanding fees are calculated from the courses you register. You can settle them securely from the Fees & Payments section of your dashboard, and a receipt is recorded against your account.
    How do I update my account details?
    Please contact the seminary office to update your name, contact details or programme information. Keeping your records current ensures you receive important notices on time.

    The seminary exists to raise ministers and members who are firmly grounded in the Scriptures and in the doctrine of the Celestial Church of Christ, equipped to serve God and humanity in truth and holiness.
